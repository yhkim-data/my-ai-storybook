**English** | [한국어](terms.ko.md)

These terms apply to everyone who uses "My AI Storybook" (the "site", <https://storybook.di-lab.io/>). Using the site means you agree to these terms.

## Article 1. No server, and keys

1. The site has no server. The site operator does not receive your key or your book.
2. A key you enter stays only in the browser's memory. It is cleared when you close the tab, reload the page, or leave the site unused for the set time.
3. Your browser sends the key only to the AI addresses of Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic.

## Article 2. Keys you may use

1. You may use only your own key, or a key its owner (a school, teacher, or parent) has allowed you to use.
2. You must not enter someone else's key without permission.
3. You must not use someone else's key that was left on a computer.
4. You must not tell your key to anyone else.

## Article 3. Charges and provider terms

1. The key owner is responsible for AI charges and usage limits.
2. The key owner is responsible for following each AI company's terms, including age requirements.
3. Key owners are advised to set a budget or usage limit on each company's own pages.

## Article 4. Shared computers

1. On a shared computer, press [Clear keys and finish] when you are done and close the browser.
2. The book you were making stays in that computer's browser. If you do not want it to stay, save it with [Save work file] and then delete the book.

## Article 5. Prohibited acts and reporting security problems

1. Attacking the site is prohibited.
2. Trying to take another person's key or book is prohibited.
3. If you find a security problem, do not make it public. Report it as described in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Article 6. Rights to the books you make

The rights to the books you make are as described in [Rights and terms of use](rights.en.md). The rights to a book belong to the person who made it.
